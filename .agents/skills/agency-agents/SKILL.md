# Agency Agents

The upstream repository `msitarzewski/agency-agents` publishes role personas
rather than installable `SKILL.md` packages. The relevant personas are vendored
in `.agents/agency-agents/` with the upstream MIT license.

## When to use

- `game-designer.md`: act structure, player fantasy, tutorial and ending copy.
- `economy-designer.md`: tariff, production and recovery balance.
- `technical-artist.md`: isometric scene composition and low-poly assets.
- `narrative-designer.md`: policy debate and faction event writing.
- `engineering-frontend-developer.md`: Solid components and browser behavior.
- `design-ui-designer.md`: hierarchy, spacing, typography and mobile layout.
- `design-ui-finish-gate-reviewer.md`: final visual finish audit.
- `engineering-software-architect.md`: layer contracts and dependency order.
- `engineering-code-reviewer.md`: independent review checklist.
- `design-ux-researcher.md`: player flow and comprehension checks.
- `engineering-technical-writer.md`: README, ADR and operator guides.
- `engineering-minimal-change-engineer.md`: scope discipline.

## Rules

A reviewer must not review work they authored. Read `AGENTS.md`,
`AGENT_DIRECTORY.md`, and the relevant persona before acting. Return evidence,
not assertions. The project's own rules override a persona when they conflict.
